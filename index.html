How a team of one ships一人團隊怎麼出貨

AI-native from the first commit 從第一行程式碼起就是 AI 原生

Every KOOKKO product is designed and coded by one founder working side by side with AI agents. That is what makes a four-product portfolio possible for a solo studio. KOOKKO 的每一款產品，都由創辦人與 AI 代理協同完成設計與開發。這正是一人工作室能同時維持四款產品的原因。

Design with AI用 AI 設計

Research, specs and UX drafts are produced with AI as the daily sparring partner, then reviewed line by line by the founder.市場研究、規格文件與互動草稿都由 AI 協同產出，再經創辦人逐段審查定稿。

Build with AI用 AI 開發

Claude is embedded in the whole engineering loop: coding, regression suites, documentation, and release checks.Claude 深入整個工程迴圈：開發、回歸測試、文件撰寫與發版檢查都有它參與。

Sell with AI靠 AI 營收

AI is the product, not a gimmick: billing is built around AI features — subscriptions, credits, and freemium tiers.AI 是產品本身而非行銷口號：收費設計圍繞 AI 功能——訂閱制、點數與免費增值分級。
